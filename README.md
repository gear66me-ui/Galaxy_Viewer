🌌 Galaxy Viewer

Galaxy Viewer grew from a fascination with the night sky that has followed me for as long as I can remember. As a child, I would point a telescope toward the stars and wonder what was really out there. Decades later, that curiosity has only grown stronger.

I’m a scientist and engineer by training, a pilot, musician, builder, and lifelong student of how things work. These days, I also proudly call myself an astronomer wannabe—captivated by the almost incomprehensible scale, beauty, and history of our universe.

Over the past several months, I’ve devoted myself to building Galaxy Viewer: an interactive application that brings together remarkable images and astronomical data from some of humanity’s great observatories and archives, including Hubble and the James Webb Space Telescope.

Behind the experience is a curated galaxy catalog connected to real astronomical coordinates and imagery, combined with an interactive sky viewer that lets you move through the universe, discover galaxies at random, explore them in high definition, and follow their source information back to the scientific archives.

My goal was simple: make exploring galaxies feel less like searching a database and more like traveling through the universe.

Galaxy Viewer is still evolving—as is my understanding of the cosmos. I hope it gives you some of the same wonder I felt looking through that telescope as a kid.

There are trillions of galaxies out there. Go explore. ✨

— Germán Arciniegas
