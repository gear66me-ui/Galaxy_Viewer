Galaxy Viewer · Development Update

New Image Providers Are Coming In

I’m adding imagery from 11 major observatories, space missions, and astronomical archives to Galaxy Viewer: James Webb / STScI, NOIRLab, Spitzer, ESO, Chandra, ESA / Hubble, Euclid, Herschel, GALEX, NuSTAR, and NRAO.

These providers are becoming part of a much larger curated image collection that Galaxy Viewer can take you to directly while you explore the sky.

I’m now working through the final presentation passes, tuning each image so it feels like part of the sky instead of a rectangular photograph dropped on top of it.

That work includes per-image AVM/WCS registration, opacity and cross-fade tuning, vignetting, edge feathering and soft masks, black-level and tonal matching, local contrast control, framing, field-of-view calibration, and transition smoothing so the observatory imagery blends cleanly into the live Aladin sky background.

I’m doing that curation image by image. It is slower than simply loading a catalog, but it lets Galaxy Viewer preserve the scientific positioning while making the transition between survey imagery and the high-resolution observatory image much more natural.

At the same time I’m finishing the last navigation, high-definition viewing, provider-link, caching, and stability work. There are still a few bugs to squash, but the app is now in the final stretch and the catalog is growing quickly.

Thanks for following the project. There are a lot more galaxies and a lot more images coming. In the meantime, enjoy the demo and go explore the universe.

— Germán
