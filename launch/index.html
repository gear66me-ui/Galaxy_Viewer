GALAXY VIEWER · DEVELOPMENT UPDATE

Almost Ready for Launch

Hi everyone — I wanted to share a quick update on Galaxy Viewer. I’ve had some major breakthroughs in the programming recently, and the app has taken a huge step forward.

I’m now in the final stretch of bug fixing and polishing, and I’ll be releasing the Google Play Store app in the next couple of weeks.

I’ve expanded the astronomical image collection with NOIRLab, ESO, Spitzer, Herschel, and Chandra imagery, alongside the Hubble and James Webb collections already in Galaxy Viewer.

Over the next couple of weeks, I’ll be curating and adding a large number of new images from these observatories and archives. There is a lot more coming as I continue building out the Galaxy Viewer catalog.

One of the biggest improvements is a new image-filtering and blending system I developed so the observatory images merge much more naturally into the live sky viewer instead of looking like a picture pasted on top. I’m now going through the collection image by image, adjusting the filter and presentation settings for each one. It takes a long time, but the results are worth it.

I’ve also already added some truly spectacular new images, with many more being curated now, while continuing to improve navigation, high-definition viewing, observatory links, and overall stability.

There are still bugs to squash, but Galaxy Viewer is very close. Thanks for following the project — and in the meantime, enjoy the demo and go explore the universe. ✨

— Germán
