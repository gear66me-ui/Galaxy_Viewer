Definitions

Full-width angle and centered 250-pixel angle are measured from sky coordinates at the pixel endpoints using Aladin's angular-distance API. Pixel-per-degree and degree-per-pixel values are calculated independently for both spans. The custom cosmic scale is S(θ) = 46.5 × sin(θ/2), with θ converted to radians; the linear comparison is 46.5 × θ/180. These are comparison models, not validated cosmological distance laws.
