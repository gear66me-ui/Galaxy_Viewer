Galaxy Viewer Build 49 Is Ready

I’ve been deep in the catalog and the engine, testing one build after another until this version finally became stable enough to share. This Friends & Family preview is now locked to Build 49.

Galaxy Viewer brings together curated high-definition galaxy imagery from major observatories, space missions, and astronomical archives, then places those images over the live Aladin sky engine so the experience feels connected to the real sky.

LOCKED SNAPSHOT: GV-beta-200-013 · BUILD 0049. This page does not follow my later development updates.

The breakthrough is the registration layer: Galaxy Viewer decodes astronomical metadata and aligns HD observatory imagery over the sky engine with field-of-view calibration, orientation, blending, vignetting and cross-fading.

Thank you for being part of the journey. Tap Launch and go explore the universe.

— Germán
