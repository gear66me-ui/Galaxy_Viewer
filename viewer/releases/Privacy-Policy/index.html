GALAXY VIEWER

PRIVACY POLICY

Effective date: October 1, 2026

Galaxy Viewer is an astronomy exploration application designed to let users navigate the sky, travel to astronomical destinations, and view curated astronomical imagery from space- and ground-based observatories.

INFORMATION GALAXY VIEWER COLLECTS

Galaxy Viewer does not require an account and does not ask users to provide a name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, financial information, or other personal profile information to use the app.

Galaxy Viewer does not sell personal information and does not use personal information for targeted advertising.

INTERNET AND TECHNICAL DATA

Galaxy Viewer uses an internet connection to load astronomical data, imagery, application resources, and webpages. When the app requests content from GitHub Pages or third-party astronomy providers, those services may receive ordinary technical information that accompanies internet requests, such as an IP address, browser or WebView information, device information, request time, and requested URL. Such information is processed according to the practices and privacy policies of the service receiving the request.

THIRD-PARTY ASTRONOMICAL CONTENT

Galaxy Viewer displays or links to astronomical imagery and information associated with organizations and programs such as Hubble, the James Webb Space Telescope, Spitzer, Chandra, ESO, NOIRLab, and other astronomy providers. Provider credits are displayed within the Galaxy Viewer experience where applicable.

These organizations are independent third parties. Galaxy Viewer does not control their websites, servers, privacy practices, or content.

EXTERNAL LINKS

The app may open external provider webpages so users can learn more about an observation or organization. Once a user accesses a third-party site, that site's terms and privacy practices apply.

DATA STORAGE AND SHARING

Galaxy Viewer does not operate a user-account database and does not intentionally maintain a database of users' personal information. The app may rely on normal local application or WebView storage and caching needed to load and operate the experience.

Galaxy Viewer does not sell or rent user personal information. Technical information transmitted to external services as part of normal internet requests is handled by those external services.

CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Galaxy Viewer is an astronomy exploration application and does not knowingly solicit personal information from children. If the developer becomes aware that personal information has been collected through a Galaxy Viewer-controlled service from a child in a manner requiring deletion, appropriate steps will be taken to remove it.

SECURITY

Reasonable efforts are made to keep Galaxy Viewer and its hosted resources secure. No method of internet transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

This Privacy Policy may be updated if Galaxy Viewer's features, data practices, or legal requirements change. The current version will be posted at this URL with its effective date.

CONTACT

Questions about this Privacy Policy may be directed to the Galaxy Viewer developer through the developer contact information published on the Galaxy Viewer listing in Google Play.

Galaxy Viewer is an independent astronomy exploration application. References to astronomical organizations, observatories, missions, and providers identify sources of astronomical content and do not imply endorsement or sponsorship unless expressly stated.
